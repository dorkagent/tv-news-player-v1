About

Where the videos live

NewsDork doesn't host a single video. Every broadcast here is publicly available on the Internet Archive's TV News Archive — this site is just a free guide and player for browsing it.

Nothing is stored, copied, or re-hosted here. Pressing play streams directly from the Archive's public collections, and every card links back to its original archive.org page.

NewsDork is free and open-source software. Donations fund development of the player — never access to the streams.
